Computer System Technology (Certificate)
The Computer Systems Technology certificate program introduces students to the fundamentals of computing. In addition to a comprehensive overview of the hardware and software components, students will explore the core functions of computer systems, programming principles, operating system concepts, data processing, storage, and communication.

Bachelor of Computing Science
The Bachelor of Computing Science program gives learners a solid foundation in the technical, communication and hands-on skills needed to excel as a computing science professional. Explore a myriad of areas, including gaming, networking and communications, big data, cloud computing, database development, artificial intelligence, robotics, graphics, and mobile applications. Virtually every industry in the world makes use of computer technology and computing science graduates are essential in developing the applications that drive those industries forward.
